How to get a free API key

1) Open Google Cloud Console and create a project. 2) Enable “YouTube Data API v3”. 3) Credentials → Create credentials → API key (restrict it to YouTube Data API v3). 4) Paste it above. The key is stored only in this browser.

Note: public API access gives subscribers, views, likes and comments. Watch hours, impressions, CTR and average view duration are private to YouTube Studio, so keep entering those manually.

Monetization tracker

Use this to track which ad options you want to review as your channel grows. Subscriber milestones are reminders—not automatic unlocks. YouTube decides eligibility based on the current YPP requirements, your country, channel status, and policy review.

Enter your subscriber count (or sync your channel) to see guidance.
Open YouTube Studio ↗

Important: selecting an ad format here only records your plan. It does not change ads on YouTube. Actual ad settings must be managed in YouTube Studio after your channel has the relevant monetization access.

Monetization notes

As of Oct 2026, YouTube lists two Partner Program tiers. Fan funding & Shopping: 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 public watch hours (12 months) or 3 million Shorts views (90 days). Ad revenue: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours or 10 million Shorts views. For new applicants from 1 Feb 2027, YouTube has announced the ad-revenue bar rises to 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views. Only qualified public hours and engaged Shorts views count, and Shorts watch hours do not count toward the watch-hour path. Meeting the numbers does not guarantee approval, so confirm in Studio → Earn.
